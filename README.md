# 🧑‍💻 Portfolio.me
### *One HTML file. Zero frameworks. Unreasonable confidence.*

🔗 **Live:** [me-prabakaransr.vercel.app](https://me-prabakaransr.vercel.app)

---

## 👋 What Is This?

The personal portfolio of **Prabakaran S R**, a CSBS student from Coimbatore. It's a fast, single-page site that shows who I am, what I've built, and how to reach me, without making you scroll through a loading screen to find out.

It even has a live IST clock, so you'll always know what time it is in Coimbatore while deciding whether to hire me.

---

## ✨ What's Inside

- 🏠 **Home**: a quick intro, a few key stats, and what I'm building right now
- 🚀 **Projects**: things I've built, broken, and fixed
- 🙋 **About**: the human behind the commits
- 📄 **Resume**: a one-click download of my latest resume
- 🔗 **Connect**: GitHub, LinkedIn, and email, one click each

---

## 🛠️ Tech

| Piece | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Site** | Single `index.html` | No build step, no `node_modules`, no drama |
| **Hosting** | Vercel | Push to `main`, site updates, I go back to debugging something else |
